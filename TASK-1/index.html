About Us

About InAmigos Foundation

InAmigos Foundation was founded on 23 September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla, Founder and CEO. Based in Bilaspur, Chhattisgarh, the foundation operates through a network of professionals and volunteers working on social-development initiatives across India.

Our mission is to address social challenges through sustainable, inclusive and community-driven action — focusing on education, community welfare, women’s empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability, employability, volunteering and social inclusion. We believe lasting change happens when communities, volunteers and organisations commit to collective action.
